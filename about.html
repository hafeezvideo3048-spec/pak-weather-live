About Pak Weather Live

Pak Weather Live is a free informational website that provides live and accurate weather data for cities across Pakistan using the trusted OpenWeatherMap API.

Our mission is to make reliable weather information easily accessible for everyone — from travelers and students to farmers and business owners. We ensure data accuracy and simplicity so visitors can plan their day with confidence.

This platform is built for public awareness and is fully compliant with Google AdSense content policies. It does not promote, sell, or misrepresent any service — it simply delivers useful information.
