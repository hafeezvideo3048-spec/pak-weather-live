About Pak Weather Live

Who We Are

Pak Weather Live is an independent public information platform that provides accurate, real-time weather updates for every major city in Pakistan. We use trusted sources like OpenWeatherMap to show temperature, humidity, rainfall, and wind data — updated every few minutes.

Our mission is to make weather information freely available, simple to understand, and accessible for everyone — from farmers and students to travelers and citizens across Pakistan.

This website is non-commercial and educational in nature, built for weather awareness and convenience.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan’s most reliable online weather resource by providing clean, ad-safe, and transparent information that helps people make better daily decisions.
