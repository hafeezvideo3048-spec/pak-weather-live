Privacy Policy

At Pak Weather Live, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any data and information that you provide or that is collected automatically through our website. We aim to provide a transparent and trustworthy experience to all our users in Pakistan and globally.

Pak Weather Live does not collect or store personal information such as names, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses, except for the email you voluntarily use to contact us. The only information the website uses is the city name entered to fetch weather data via the OpenWeatherMap API. This data is non-personal and is used strictly to provide accurate weather forecasts.

We do not employ cookies, tracking pixels, or other forms of automated tracking tools. Your browsing activity on our website remains private. Any ads served by Google AdSense may use non-personalized cookies in accordance with Google’s advertising policies, but we do not influence or control how Google collects this data. For more details, please refer to Google Ad Policies.

All weather data displayed on Pak Weather Live is sourced from trusted third-party APIs, primarily OpenWeatherMap. We ensure that the information provided is accurate, up-to-date, and publicly available. This means you can safely use our website without concerns about data misuse or privacy violations.

We provide guidance on how users can interact with our website safely, including avoiding sharing personal information publicly. Our platform is designed to be informational and educational, offering insights into Pakistan’s weather patterns, forecasts, and climate awareness.

When you contact us via email, your message and any personal information included are used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not share your contact information with any third parties, and we handle your messages with strict confidentiality.

Our website is fully compliant with Google AdSense content policies. All content, including text, images, and advertisements, is original, high-quality, and designed to provide informational value. This ensures a safe experience for our users and avoids issues related to low-value or copied content.

Pak Weather Live is committed to educating users about privacy and responsible online behavior. We encourage users to review our policies and understand how their data is handled. We also provide transparency about our sources, services, and third-party integrations.

Our long-term goal is to maintain a trustworthy, informative platform for all Pakistanis seeking accurate weather information. By not collecting personal data and limiting all usage to essential information for weather forecasts, we ensure that your online experience is secure and private.
