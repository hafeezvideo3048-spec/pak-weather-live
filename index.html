Pakistan Weather Updates — Live & Accurate

Pak Weather Live provides the most accurate, real-time weather information for all cities in Pakistan. Whether you live in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, or Quetta — we keep you updated with temperature, humidity, and rainfall alerts every few minutes.

This website uses verified data sources including OpenWeather API and presents results in a user-friendly format. Our goal is to make weather information freely accessible and useful for students, travelers, and citizens across Pakistan.

The information shared here is for general awareness only and should not be used for aviation or emergency purposes. For official alerts, please refer to the Pakistan Meteorological Department.
