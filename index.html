About Weather in Pakistan

Pakistan experiences a diverse climate — from the hot plains of Sindh to the snow-covered peaks of Gilgit Baltistan. The summer season brings monsoon rains, while winters offer cool breezes and occasional snowfall in the north.

Our aim is to provide authentic, daily, and accessible weather updates so you can plan your travels, farming, and daily life effectively.
