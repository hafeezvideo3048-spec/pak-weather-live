About Weather in Pakistan

Pakistan has diverse climates ranging from snow-capped mountains in the north to coastal areas in the south. Each city experiences unique weather conditions that impact daily life, agriculture, and travel planning. Understanding the weather is essential for planning, safety, and lifestyle decisions.

Northern regions like Gilgit-Baltistan and Skardu experience extremely cold winters and mild summers, while southern cities such as Karachi face hot, humid conditions year-round. Monsoon season brings rain to central and southern Pakistan, which is vital for agriculture but can also lead to flooding.

Pak Weather Live aims to provide reliable, real-time updates for all regions, including temperature, humidity, wind, and rainfall forecasts. Our platform ensures users receive accurate and timely information to plan daily activities effectively.

Our forecasts are powered by OpenWeatherMap API and other trusted sources. This allows users to view current weather conditions, as well as a seven-day outlook for temperature, precipitation, and wind. Each forecast card provides clear and concise information for easy understanding.

Climate change has introduced unpredictable patterns in Pakistan, making accurate forecasts even more crucial. Extreme heat, sudden rain, and storms are now more frequent, impacting daily life and agriculture. We strive to provide early warnings and alerts for safer planning.

Our 7-day forecast system provides users with a complete overview of upcoming weather conditions, including daily highs and lows, humidity levels, and a graphical representation of expected rainfall. This makes it easier for households, businesses, and travelers to prepare.

Beyond temperature and rain, we offer guidance on seasonal changes, local weather anomalies, and general climate information. Users can check daily weather updates, trends, and warnings for their selected cities.
